What this dataset contains

California's historic fire perimeters of 1,000 acres or more, stacked in 3D by decade: the 1950s at the bottom, the 2020s on top. See where fires have returned decade after decade. Each fire keeps its real footprint, so places that burned again and again build up into tall stacks. Play it in Cinematic mode with the Strata timeline effect to watch each decade land on the last.
